NoNor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The short version

NoNor is a tool for your own plans, food, shopping and workouts. What you put into it stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account unless you choose to send it. We have no accounts. There are no ads and no tracking, and we never sell anything about you or share it for advertising.

Three things can leave NoNor for us or for the company behind our AI, and only because you chose them:

  • Usage counts — how often features are used, as daily totals, if you said yes when NoNor asked. Never what you wrote, cooked, weigh or are allergic to. You can change your mind at any time.
  • A problem report or an idea — only when you write one and tap Send. You see everything it contains before it goes.
  • What you send to NoNor's AI — only if you use it. NoNor asks you first, says what goes, and you can stop at any time.

Usage counts and reports are encrypted so that only NoNor Pro can read them. Everything else is still yours alone.

A few features have to ask the internet for something — looking up a barcode, opening a recipe link, drawing a walking route. When they do, they send only what that request needs, and this policy lists every one of them.

Who we are

NoNor is published by NoNor Pro — the name under which André Cruz, an individual developer in Sweden, publishes the app. Questions about privacy: support@nonorpro.com.

For the things that can reach us or that we pass on for you — usage counts, problem reports or ideas you send, and what you send to NoNor's AI — NoNor Pro is the data controller. For everything else in NoNor we are not, because none of it ever reaches us. Usage counts and reports travel through Apple's iCloud, which acts as our processor for them; Apple's own privacy terms apply to that carriage. For NoNor's AI, Anthropic and our hosting company act as our processors.

Where your information is kept

  • On your iPhone and Apple Watch. Everything you create or record in NoNor — plans, recipes, grocery lists, your pantry, workouts and their routes, notes, photos, exercise videos and documents you import — is stored by the app on your device.
  • In your own iCloud account, if iCloud is turned on for NoNor. The app uses Apple's iCloud to back up your data and keep your iPhone and other devices in step. It is stored in your private iCloud space, under your Apple Account, and Apple's iCloud terms apply. We have no access to it. You can turn this off in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.
  • Names of the people in your household are additionally encrypted on your device, with a key kept in your iCloud Keychain.
  • Apple Health. If you allow it, NoNor reads and writes health and fitness data in Apple Health (see below). That data is managed by Apple Health under your control.

We do not have a copy of any of this, so we cannot recover it for you if it is deleted.

What leaves your device, and why

Every internet request also shows the receiving server your device's IP address, as any app or website does.

  • Scan or search for a product — the barcode, or the words you searched for, go to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org and its sister databases for household, beauty and pet products).
  • Import a recipe from a link — a normal request for that web page and its photo goes to the website you linked to.
  • Plan a walking or running route — the map area around the route you are planning goes to public OpenStreetMap servers, which return the paths and roads in that area.
  • Look at a map — map requests go to Apple Maps.
  • Dictate a grocery list — your voice is processed on your iPhone; only for a language without an on-device voice model does it go to Apple's speech recognition service.
  • Use iCloud sync — your NoNor data goes to your own iCloud account, run by Apple.
  • Share usage counts, if you said yes — one encrypted record a day goes to us through iCloud (see below).
  • Send a problem report or an idea — what you wrote and chose goes to us, encrypted, through iCloud (see below).
  • Use NoNor's AI — what it needs to answer you goes to Anthropic through our server (see NoNor's AI below).

Some features run on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device models, and nothing you ask those is sent anywhere. NoNor's AI is different: it runs in the cloud.

NoNor's AI

NoNor's AI runs on Claude, made by Anthropic PBC. It is your choice. If you say no, the rest of NoNor keeps working.

Nothing is sent until you agree. NoNor asks before the first time. It asks separately before using your health details, and separately again before reading pages you bring in.

  • What goes when you use it: what you type, recipes and workouts from your library, your plans with anyone's name covered, your workout days, level and equipment, recent workouts as words, the meals, water, workouts, groceries and pantry you ask about, and words to search the web when NoNor has nothing.
  • Your health details, only if you allow them: your allergies, diet, foods you avoid, goal, NoNor's targets and supplements, and the same for the people at home, as Person 2, 3 and never their names.
  • Pages you bring in, only if you allow them: photos or documents such as a coach's plan. They can hold names, health details and medicines. NoNor covers the names it can find, so check them before you send.
  • What NoNor never adds: your weight, height, age, heart rate, routes, places, notes or anyone's name. If you type something yourself, it is sent as you typed it.

Why. Only to answer you and do what you asked. For people in the EU, EEA and UK, the legal basis is your consent, and your explicit consent for health details.

Where it goes. It is processed in the United States by Anthropic, reached through a small server we run with a hosting company. That server checks your subscription and counts your AI allowance. The United States may not protect your information in the same way your own country does. For people in the EU, EEA and UK, the transfer relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum), because the EU has no adequacy decision that covers it.

How long it is kept.

  • Our server keeps a conversation in progress for up to an hour so the AI can carry on with it, then deletes it. It also keeps how much of your allowance you have used, an identifier for your App Store subscription and one for your device. Its logs hold numbers and those identifiers, never what you or the AI wrote.
  • Anthropic deletes what it receives within 30 days. If something is flagged for misuse, it can keep it for up to 2 years and its safety scores for up to 7. Anthropic does not use it to train AI.

Stopping. Settings → NoNor's AI lets you withdraw each consent, as easily as you gave it. Nothing more is sent after that. What was already sent is deleted as described above. To have what our server holds deleted, write to support@nonorpro.com.

Usage counts

NoNor asks you once — on a page called Help improve NoNor — whether you are willing to share usage counts. Nothing is counted unless you say yes, and saying no is a normal answer, not a reduced version of the app.

  • What is counted: which screens and features you open, and where something goes wrong. Each one is a fixed label chosen by us in the app's own code — "opened Recipes", "import failed" — and a running total for the day.
  • What is never counted: anything you typed, cooked, imported, photographed or measured. No names, no recipes, no notes, no photos, no weights, no heart rates, no allergies. The app is built so that these cannot be put into a count even by mistake: a count can only carry a label from that fixed list, never free text.
  • How it reaches us: as one record a day, encrypted on your iPhone so that only NoNor Pro can open it, carried by your own iCloud. It is not linked to your name or e-mail. It does travel under your Apple Account, so we ask you rather than claiming it is anonymous.
  • How long we keep it: the day's totals are added into our overall figures and the record is then deleted.
  • Changing your mind: Settings → Help & feedback → Share usage counts. Turning it off stops the counting and deletes anything counted on your device that has not been sent.

We use this only to decide what to improve. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never combined with anything else about you.

Problem reports and ideas

If something goes wrong, you can send us a report from Settings → Help & feedback. You can also send an idea or a feature request the same way. Nothing is sent unless you write it and tap Send.

  • What it contains: your own words; any screenshots you picked yourself; and whichever of the listed extras you left switched on — your NoNor and iPhone version, language and region, free space, and counts of how many plans, recipes or workouts you have (how many, never what they are). You can read the whole thing before you send it — the report page has a See exactly what's sent screen that shows the complete contents.
  • The activity log is optional and off. If we cannot reproduce a problem, the app can record what NoNor itself does — screens opened, steps of an import, error codes — while you make the problem happen again. It records what the app did, never what you have. It only runs if you turn it on, it stops by itself after 24 hours or when you send the report, it is limited in size, and it never leaves your device unless you send it with a report.
  • Crash reports. If NoNor closes unexpectedly, iOS hands the app a technical report about the crash the next time you open it. NoNor keeps it in memory only, never writes it to storage, and offers once to attach it to a report. If you say no it is discarded. Separately, if you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with developers, Apple sends us its own anonymous crash statistics; those contain none of your NoNor data.
  • An e-mail address only if you add one, and only so replies can also reach you there. Replies otherwise appear in the app.
  • Encryption: a report is encrypted on your iPhone before it leaves, so that only NoNor Pro can open it. Our reply is encrypted so that only your devices can open it. Reports travel through Apple's iCloud; nobody else, including anyone who can see that those records exist, can read them.
  • How long: we delete a report and its replies six months after it is closed, and you can delete yours at any time from Settings → Help & feedback → Your reports. Deleting it also destroys the key on your device, so a later reply can never be opened by anyone.
  • Ideas: if you send us an idea or a feature request, we may use it to improve NoNor without owing you anything for it. An idea travels light — it carries your words, your screenshots and the NoNor version, and nothing else.

Please do not put health details in a report unless you want us to read them. We never ask for them, and nothing in a report is collected automatically about your body, your health or what you eat.

Permissions

NoNor asks for each of these only when a feature needs it, through Apple's own permission prompts. You can change any of them in iOS Settings at any time; the rest of the app keeps working.

  • Apple Health — to read heart rate, energy, distance, workouts, routes, Activity rings and mobility measurements, and to save the workouts you record.
  • Location — to record the route of a workout, including while the screen is off during that workout, and to plan routes.
  • Camera — to scan barcodes and receipts, photograph recipes, and film your own exercise clips. Recognition happens on your device.
  • Photos — only the photos or videos you pick.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — to dictate groceries.
  • Motion & Fitness — steps and cadence during a workout.
  • Bluetooth — to connect heart-rate straps, foot pods, power meters and gym machines you choose.

Health information

Some of what you keep in NoNor is about your health: workouts and heart rate, your height and weight, goals, allergies, diets, supplements. We treat all of it as sensitive. It is never used for advertising or marketing and never sold.

We do not receive it unless you send it. Usage counts cannot carry it, and a problem report gathers only versions and counts of how many things you have. There are two exceptions. NoNor's AI receives the health details you allow it to use, as described above. And if you write a weight or a diagnosis into a report, we will read it, because you wrote it to us. That is why the report page asks you not to unless you mean to, and shows you everything before it is sent.

Data NoNor reads from Apple Health is used only to show you your own training and progress. Your health information is also stored encrypted in your own iCloud, where only you can read it.

US state health-data laws

Some US states (for example Washington, Nevada and Connecticut) have laws about "consumer health data". Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, in Settings, says what applies and how to use your rights. We never sell consumer health data.

Other people's information

If you add household members — their names, diets or allergies — you are keeping that information in your own app for your own household. Please only add what they are happy for you to keep, and only let NoNor's AI use details of the people at home if they are happy with that too. It receives them as Person 2, 3 and never their names.

Children

NoNor is not meant for anyone under 18, and NoNor asks you to confirm your age. We do not knowingly collect information from a child. If you let NoNor's AI use details of a child at home, it receives their age group, diet and allergies without their name, and only a parent or guardian should allow that.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is with you and not with us, you are in control directly:

  • See and change it — everything is in the app.
  • Delete it — delete any item in the app. To delete everything: delete NoNor from your devices, then delete NoNor's data from iCloud (iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → NoNor → Delete). Workouts saved to Apple Health are removed in the Health app.
  • Take it with you — workouts can be exported as GPX or TCX files from the app, and Apple Health can export its own data.
  • Stop syncing — turn off iCloud for NoNor in iOS Settings.
  • Withdraw your consent to usage counts — Settings → Help & feedback → Share usage counts, at any time, as easily as you gave it. It stops immediately and what is on your device is deleted.
  • Delete a report you sent — Settings → Help & feedback → Your reports.
  • Stop using NoNor's AI — Settings → NoNor's AI, at any time.

If you live in the EU, EEA or UK you have rights under data protection law (such as access, correction, erasure and complaint). For almost everything in NoNor we cannot act on those rights for you, because we do not hold the data — you do, and the app and your iCloud settings are where you exercise them. For what we do hold — usage counts you agreed to, reports you sent, and what our AI server keeps — write to support@nonorpro.com and we will find it, send it to you or delete it. Wherever you live, you can ask.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority; in Sweden that is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.

If something goes wrong

If a security problem exposes your information, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If NoNor starts doing anything new with your information — for example an account, a subscription or a cloud feature — this policy will change first, and the app will show you the new version before that feature is used.
