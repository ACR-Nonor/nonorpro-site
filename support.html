Support

Write to support@nonorpro.com and a person will answer. Tell us which device you are on, the version of NoNor (Settings, at the bottom) and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

What does it cost?

NoNor is free for 14 days, without its AI. You start the trial through Apple, and unless you cancel it renews into a paid plan: NoNor Plus or NoNor Pro, both with AI. Upgrade during the trial to get the AI at once; that ends the free days and starts your paid plan. Prices will be on this page before launch.

Do I have to log what I eat?

No. Following the plan is the whole job. NoNor keeps a quiet record of what your plan served and what you trained, there when you want to look and easy to hide when you don't.

Does everyone in my household need an iPhone?

No. Add the people you cook for on your own phone, children included. Each gets their own day, portions and allergies, and their own plan if they need one.

Can a nutritionist or coach send me a plan?

Yes. They build the plan in NoNor and send it as a link. Open it on your iPhone and the whole plan, every recipe and session, is added to yours.

Can NoNor keep me safe from an allergen?

It warns you, for each person you cook for, and it can swap an ingredient out. It is a help, not a guarantee — always check the pack.

Where does my information live?

On your devices and in your own iCloud account. See the privacy policy.
